The demo loops, drum machine, synths and all 29 sound effects are generated in your browser, so everything works offline. The microphone channel works in the installed app, where the browser can ask for mic access. Swap any pad for your own recording with Use my own sound or by dropping a file on the pad. Load your own audio (MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC) with each deck's Load button, the button above, or by dropping files onto a deck. Knobs: drag up or down, scroll, or use arrow keys. Double-click a knob or fader to reset it. Hold Shift while dragging for fine control. Roll and Censor work while held; the track keeps its place underneath and picks up where it would have been.
